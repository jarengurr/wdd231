Site Plan

Site Name: The Mysterious Resistor

For some people new to electronics, resisters my seem mysterious at first. My goal is to make resisters less mysterious.

Site Purpose

To help hobbiest understand what resistors are and what types are available. Also, to make it easy to convert a resistor color code to a resistance value.

I will talk about the properities of resistors and what someone needs to know before picking a resistor for a circuit

Scenarios

What is a resistor?

What types of resistors are available?

What do I need to know to pick a resistor for a circuit?

How can I convert the resistors color code to numerical resistor values?
